# KepamMeter Questions

16 questions, fixed order. Each answer carries a weight; the last option on every question is the heaviest (20).

**Score** = points earned ÷ maximum possible × 100, rounded. Skipped questions are excluded from both numbers.
Maximum possible with every question answered: 320 points = 100%.

**Angry mode** (red lights, shaking, angry model, spilled matcha) starts above **40%**.

## Diagnosis bands

| Score | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| 0 to 30% | 😇 Pure Soul |
| 31 to 70% | 😌 Average Netizen |
| 71 to 100% | 🔥 Certified Kepamist |
